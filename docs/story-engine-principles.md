# Story Engine principles

Design guidance only. Story Engine is not implemented in this pass.

## Evidence before language

- No fabricated provenance, ownership, origin, maker, rarity or investment claims.
- Facts before prose. Generate from structured facts, never empty style prompts.
- Every important claim must trace to a certificate, attributed jeweller statement, provenance record or relevant research source.
- Preserve source references, dates, confidence and verification status through story generation. A jeweller statement remains attributed until independently verified; missing evidence stays missing.
- AI may phrase supported facts. It must not invent facts or turn uncertainty into certainty.
- Prefer concrete details over adjectives. Reject generic phrases such as "timeless elegance".

## Separate outputs

1. Boutique Caption: concise, factual product context.
2. Signature Story: focused narrative supported by the piece's evidence.
3. Collector Story: documented material, craft and historical context.
4. SAME Story: conversational explanation tailored to the customer's question.
5. Voice Script: speakable wording with the same evidential limits.
6. Collector Dossier: structured claims, source references and unresolved questions.

Tone: museum curator + auction specialist + private concierge. Clear, measured and specific; no invented prestige or generic luxury rhetoric.

## Examples

These are illustrative examples, not facts about AMES inventory.

BAD: "A timeless masterpiece of unrivalled elegance, born from centuries of royal heritage."

GOOD, only when the supplied evidence supports it: "The supplied grading report records a 1.20 ct oval diamond, G colour and VS1 clarity. The jeweller describes the setting as platinum. No previous ownership history has been supplied."

BAD: "An ethically sourced treasure that will appreciate for generations."

GOOD: "The jeweller supplied a material invoice and grading report. Neither establishes the stone's mine of origin or future value; those claims are omitted."

Structured input should keep each fact alongside its source reference, attribution and confidence. Outputs must retain that linkage even when source details are presented in the dossier rather than the short caption.
